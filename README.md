# Numu Growth Ethics Gate Lite

A plugin for Claude. Check anything you're about to send or publish, such as marketing, a proposal, a client report, a fundraising letter, a policy or a job advert. A self-check that quotes the lines that use manipulative tactics such as fake urgency or hidden fees and asks seven questions of your text.

The plugin is in the [numu-growth-ethics-gate-lite](numu-growth-ethics-gate-lite) folder and its [README](numu-growth-ethics-gate-lite/README.md) says what it does and doesn't do. Outside that folder is scripts/build.sh, the tool Numu Growth uses to build, check and tag a release. It is not part of the plugin.
