# Numu Growth Ethics Gate Lite

## What it is

An Agent Skill. It reads anything you're about to send or publish, such as marketing, a proposal, a client report, a fundraising letter, a policy or a job advert, and gives you three things:

- the lines that use a known manipulative tactic, quoted, with a fix for each
- what your text shows against five of its seven questions
- the other two questions, which only you can answer, handed back to you

## What it isn't

- It isn't a review, an audit, a certification or advice.
- It doesn't give scores, ratings or badges.
- It doesn't check the law, advertising codes or platform rules.
- It doesn't check how the work was made or what it's for. It looks only at the text you give it.

It's one model's reading of your text. It will miss things and it can be wrong.

## How to use it

Ask Claude to check your text before it goes out, for example "Check this landing page for dodgy tactics", "Is this fundraising letter fair?" or "Check this job advert before I post it" and paste the text itself. A description can't be checked line by line. When this plugin checks your text, its findings sit between a header starting AUTOMATED SELF-CHECK and a footer ending "Made by Numu Growth (numugrowth.ai)". If you get findings without them, the answer is Claude's own: ask again and name the plugin. The findings list the lines to change, what the text shows against the questions a reading can answer, and the two questions only you can answer.

## Your data

This plugin runs no code and sends nothing to Numu Growth or anyone else. It is a set of written instructions with no hooks, servers or connectors. The one script in its repository, scripts/build.sh, is the tool Numu Growth uses to build, check and tag a release. Nothing in the plugin calls it, so it never runs when you use the plugin. The text you check is handled by the AI app you run it in, such as Claude. That app's privacy terms apply.
