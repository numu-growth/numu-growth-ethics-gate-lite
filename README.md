# Numu Growth Ethics Gate Lite

**Status: not for release.** This draft hasn't been through the approvals, reviews or gate runs it needs before it's published.

## What it is

An Agent Skill. It reads anything you're about to send or publish, such as marketing, a proposal, a client report, a fundraising letter, a policy or a job advert, and gives you three things:

- the lines that use a known manipulative tactic, quoted, with a fix for each
- what your text shows against seven questions
- the two questions only you can answer, handed back to you

## What it isn't

- It isn't a review, an audit, a certification or advice.
- It doesn't give scores, ratings or badges.
- It doesn't check the law, advertising codes or platform rules.
- It doesn't check how the work was made, or what it's for. It checks tactics only.

It's one model's reading of your text. It will miss things and it can be wrong.

## How to use it

Ask Claude to check your text before it goes out, for example "Check this landing page for dodgy tactics", "Is this fundraising letter fair?" or "Check this job advert before I post it" and paste the text itself. A description can't be checked line by line. The findings list the lines to change, what the text shows against the questions a reading can answer, and the two questions only you can answer.

## Your data

This plugin has no code and sends nothing to Numu Growth. The text you check goes only to the AI app you run it in, such as Claude, and that app's privacy terms apply.
